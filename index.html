Who we are

Every life is precious to Godअनमोल जीवन

The name says it plainly: Anmol Jeevan means precious life. We exist to carry the hope of Jesus into homes, streets, and screens across India — in the language people pray and dream in.

What began as a passion to proclaim Christ's love has grown into a daily rhythm of teaching, worship, and testimony — reaching a growing family online and gathering together each week in Indore.

Whether you've walked with Jesus for years or you're simply curious, there is a place for you here.

You may also know us as AJM, AJM Family, or AJMI.

Frequently asked questions

What does AJM stand for?

AJM stands for Anmol Jeevan Ministries — also known as AJM Family or AJMI — a Hindi-language Christian ministry based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India.

Who is Apostle Vishal Samuel?

Apostle Vishal Samuel, also known as Pastor Vishal Samuel, is the founder and lead pastor of Anmol Jeevan Ministries (AJM). He leads the ministry's teaching, worship, and vision, with a desire to proclaim the love of Jesus to all nations.

Who is Pastor Juhi Vishal?

Pastor Juhi Vishal serves alongside Apostle Vishal Samuel at Anmol Jeevan Ministries, pouring into the AJM Family through ministry, worship, and pastoral care.

Is AJM Family the same as Anmol Jeevan Ministries?

Yes. AJM Family, AJM, and AJMI are all names people use for Anmol Jeevan Ministries — the same Hindi-language ministry led by Apostle Vishal Samuel and Pastor Juhi Vishal.

Where is Anmol Jeevan Ministries located?

Anmol Jeevan Ministries (AJM) is based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India, with a weekly in-person Sunday gathering plus daily online prayer meetings on Zoom.

How can I join a gathering with the AJM Family?

You're welcome any Sunday for worship, or join our Zoom prayer meetings during the week. Visit our Events page to reserve your spot or get the Zoom link.
